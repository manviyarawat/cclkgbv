Tracker Rows Maker

How to use (every session):
  1. Download the state-wise export (ccl-state-wise-....xlsx) and choose it in step 1.
  2. Pick the session, and type Sr.No, Responsibility and Facilitator (step 2).
  3. Check the preview in step 3, then click Copy rows.
  4. In your Google tracker sheet, click the Sr.No cell of the "Total" row for this session and press Ctrl+V. All 9 rows fill in at once.
  5. Fill "Actual activity photos" and "Actual challenge question updated" by hand, as before.

Notes: numbers for past sessions keep growing as late forms and uploads arrive, so a file downloaded later shows slightly higher numbers – that is normal. "Had some" material is calculated as: Attendance form sent − Had all − Had none.
