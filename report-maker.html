Session Report Maker

How to use (every session):
  1. Fill steps 1 to 6 from top to bottom. Your typing is saved automatically.
  2. Step 2: write rough notes → click Copy request for Claude → paste in a Claude chat → paste Claude's three sections into the boxes.
  3. Add images (snippets and poll charts) last – images are not auto-saved.
  4. Click Make my report. A Word file downloads.
  5. Upload it to Google Drive → double-click → Open with Google Docs.
  6. For the next session, click Start new report at the bottom.
